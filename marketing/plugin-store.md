Postie connects Craft Commerce with shipping carriers. Fetch live rates at checkout, create shipments and labels, and keep tracking information available to the store and its customers.

Postie supports more than one carrier, and each provider can add a flat or percentage allowance for packing and handling. Use the rate tester to check parcel details and returned services before they reach checkout.

## Features

- Offer live shipping rates from supported carriers during checkout.
- Connect multiple providers and carrier services within the same store.
- Test parcel details and returned rates from the control panel.
- Add a flat or percentage allowance for packing and handling.
- Pack items separately, into one box or across configured and provider-supplied boxes.
- Find products and variants that are missing dimensions or weight.
- Create complete or partial shipments from Craft Commerce orders.
- Generate shipping labels and store tracking numbers where the provider supports them.
- Update orders to the Shipped or Partially Shipped status as shipments are created.
- Retrieve tracking status and delivery progress from supported providers.
- Configure providers through the control panel or a project configuration file.
- Inspect rate requests and errors through the Craft debug toolbar.
- Register custom providers and adjust shipping behaviour through plugin events.
