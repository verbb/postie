Postie connects Craft Commerce to the carriers that fulfil the order. Quote live rates, add your packing margin, create shipments and labels, and keep tracking information available to the store and its customers.

The rate at checkout should reflect what the store is actually sending. Connect supported carriers for live quotes, find products missing required dimensions and add an allowance for packing and handling.

## Features

- **Live carrier rates:** Offer checkout methods based on current provider quotes.
- **Multiple providers:** Connect different carriers and services within the same store.
- **Shipping labels:** Create shipments and generate provider labels from order data.
- **Tracking helpers:** Expose carrier-aware tracking information after fulfilment.
- **Partial shipments:** Lodge selected order line items and quantities as stock becomes ready.
- **Order status updates:** Move Commerce orders through the appropriate fulfilment status when shipments are created.
- **Tracking statuses:** Retrieve delivery progress from supported carriers.
- **Rate markups:** Add a flat or percentage packing allowance to returned rates.
- **Rates testing:** Verify parcel details and quotes from the control panel.
- **Flexible packing:** Pack items separately, use one parcel, or distribute the order across defined boxes.
- **Provider boxes:** Use standard carrier packaging where the provider makes it available.
- **Volume-based packing:** Account for product dimensions and volume when matching items to boxes.
- **From checkout to delivery:** Create carrier shipments and labels from Craft, expose tracking information to customers and investigate quotes before they affect a live checkout.
